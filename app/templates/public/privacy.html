This policy explains how The Mine 606 handles information when you visit themine606.com or send us a request through the site.

Information you provide

When you submit a private party inquiry, we receive your name, email address, event date, and any details you provide, such as your phone number, party size, preferred area, or message. When you apply to play at The Mine, we receive your name, email address, and any phone number, genre, performance link, or message you include.

How we use and keep it

We use submissions to respond, review event and performance requests, and keep track of arrangements. Submissions are stored in the website database and available to staff who manage inquiries. We may use website hosting and database providers to operate these features or disclose information when required by law. We keep submissions as needed to handle requests, maintain business records, and meet legal obligations.

Browser storage and external services

Your browser stores your Day / Night preference on your device. Staff sign-in uses a session cookie. Our hosting service may record technical information, such as IP addresses and request details, to operate and troubleshoot the site.

The site uses Google Fonts and embeds a Google map on the location page. Loading these resources or images from an external host may share technical request information with the provider. If you follow a map, social media, or event ticket link, that service handles your visit under its own privacy policy.

Your choices

You can browse the menus without submitting a form, and you can clear the site’s saved preference and cookies in your browser settings. Contact us if you want to ask about, correct, or request deletion of information you submitted. We may need to verify the request and retain some records for business or legal reasons.
